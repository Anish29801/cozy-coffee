The first sip of the day is never yours alone. We line up — barista, baker, dishwasher, regular who arrived early — and taste the same cup. If it's off, we say so. If it's good, we say so louder.

That ritual is the whole café: **togetherness over perfection.**

Today's dial: 18g in, 36g out, 27s. Ethiopia Guji, washed. Tastes like orange peel and brown sugar. Come taste it with us tomorrow at 7am — first sip's on the house.
