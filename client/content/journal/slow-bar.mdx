There is a table in the corner with no outlet. We did it on purpose.

In a world of fast wifi and faster lattes, we wanted one place where you are allowed to be slow. Bring a book. Watch the rain. Talk to a stranger.

It's the most requested table. We think you know why.

> "Your usual table is waiting." — we mean it.
